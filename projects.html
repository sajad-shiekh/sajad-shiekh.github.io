Featured Projects

Electron Monte Carlo Beam Modeling (Varian Eclipse TPS)

Commissioned and validated the updated eMC dose-calculation algorithm in Eclipse. Performed 3D water-tank scanning, applicator modeling, cutout-specific beam modeling, and cross-checks with historical beam data.

Machine Learning for Clinical Risk Prediction

Built ML models (LogReg, RF, GBM) to predict one-year hospital mortality using admission, demographic, and diagnostic data. Completed full workflow: training, calibration, tuning, and explainability.

Plasmonic Nanocavity Raman Platform for Cancer Biomarkers

Developed MOS₂ plasmonic nanocavity system for enhanced Raman spectroscopy of proteoliposomes, combined with ML-based spectral classification. Published in npj 2D Materials and Applications.

Single-Molecule Biophysics of Telomeric DNA

Extensive work on G-quadruplexes, telomeric overhangs, and DNA–protein interactions using FRET-PAINT, smFRET, and cryo-EM. Published in PNAS, Nature Communications, NAR, JACS, and more.
