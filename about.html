About Me

I am currently completing the Graduate Diploma in Medical Radiation Physics (CAMPEP) at McGill University (2025–2026), where I gained hands-on experience in linac QA, TG-51 dosimetry, small-field dosimetry, IMRT QA, HDR brachytherapy calibration, and diagnostic imaging QC.

Before transitioning into medical physics, I earned my Ph.D. in Physics at Kent State University (2018–2023), specializing in single-molecule biophysics, nanofluidics, and advanced microscopy. My work contributed to publications in PNAS, Nature Communications, Nucleic Acids Research, JACS, and Science Advances.

At McGill, I expanded into cancer biomarker research, microfluidics, plasmonics, and machine-learning-based spectral classification.

I am driven by a simple goal: use physics, computation, and engineering to solve clinically meaningful problems.
